HARDWARE HACKING · 2025

iMac firmware password removal via SPI flash

A client's iMac was locked by a firmware password, and they asked me to remove it. I opened the iMac, found the SPI flash chip on the logic board, dumped it in-circuit with a CH341A programmer and a SOIC-8 clip, cleared the section that stores the lock, then wrote the patched image back and verified it.

  1. Open the iMac and locate the SPI flash (MXIC MX25L6473E)
  2. Dump the firmware in-circuit and keep a backup
  3. Clear the firmware-lock section in the dump
  4. Erase, write back the patched image and verify

Lesson: on older Intel Macs the firmware password sits in a flash chip that anyone with physical access and a cheap programmer can read and rewrite. Physical access beats most software locks, which is why Apple later moved this into a dedicated security chip.
